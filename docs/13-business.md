# Business

A commercial product with a free tier and a subscription, starting in the Estonian market. Everything undecided is listed under [open questions](../SPEC.md#open-questions).

## Free and paid

| | Free | Paid |
|---|------|------|
| Logging, templates, offline | Yes | Yes |
| Reminders | Yes | Yes |
| Goals and body weight | Yes | Yes |
| History, export, account deletion | Yes | Yes |
| Headline progress for each activity | Yes | Yes |
| Full charts, long periods, training volume, activity detail | | Yes |

Logging and the first sense of progress stay free, because people stopping logging is the product's main risk.

The exact line still needs deciding in M4, for example whether personal records and the 1-month chart are free.

## Timing

Payments go live **soon after public launch**, once retention has been shown. Until then everything is available to everyone.

The build prepares for this in one way only: every advanced progress view goes through a single access check, which allows everything for now. No payment code is written before M7.
